Swile Enroll

Connect Swile to Budget Forecaster so it can sync on its own.

Installation

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Swile Enroll

Usage

1 Log in to team.swile.co
2 Click the "Swile Enroll" bookmarklet; it copies your token to the clipboard
3 Paste the token into Budget Forecaster → Settings → Swile
